About the App

What is DripTail?

DripTail is a mobile fashion shopping app that lets customers browse trendy clothing, save with vouchers, and place and track orders with ease.

01Browse

Explore Men’s and Women’s collections with photos and peso prices.

02Shop & Save

Add to cart, keep favorites, and apply SAVE5 to SAVE25 vouchers.

03Order & Track

Pay by Cash on Delivery, follow your order, and cancel within 10 minutes.

The Problem

Shopping for clothes online shouldn’t be a struggle.

01Hard to browse

Products are spread out, with unclear categories.

02Unclear savings

Discounts are hard to find and totals appear late.

03Complicated checkout

Long forms and few payment options cause drop-offs.

04No order control

Orders are hard to follow or cancel in time.

Objectives

What DripTail sets out to do.

General objective

To develop DripTail, a mobile fashion shopping app that makes browsing, buying, and managing clothing orders simple and convenient.

1Provide secure login, registration, and account profiles.Accounts
2Present Men’s and Women’s collections in an easy-to-browse catalog.Browse
3Let customers add items to a cart and save favorites.Cart
4Simplify checkout with Cash on Delivery and voucher discounts.Checkout
5Allow order tracking and cancellation within 10 minutes.Orders
